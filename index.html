    How the numbers work

    We compare two simple annualized hypothetical interest streams. The candidate earns nothing during your specified delay. We subtract your USD gas, bridge, swap, withdrawal, future exit and price-impact estimates. Reward incentives can be haircut from 0 to 100%. No compounding or tax treatment is modeled.

    Check rates at the source — not here

    This free calculator intentionally has no market-rate API. A source dashboard is a starting point, not a guarantee or an audit.
